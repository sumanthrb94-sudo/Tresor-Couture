Tresor Couture — Firebase Auth subject lines
=============================================

Password reset
  Tresor Couture — reset your password

Email verification
  Tresor Couture — confirm your email

Email address change
  Tresor Couture — confirm your new email address

SMS (phone OTP) — Firebase sends this automatically; the SMS body is
configured in the same Templates screen. Recommended copy:
  Your Tresor Couture verification code is %LOGIN_CODE%. It expires in 10 minutes.
